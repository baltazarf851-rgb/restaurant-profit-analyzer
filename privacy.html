Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Summary

The current version of Restaurant Profit Analyzer does not require an account and does not send the product data you enter to us or to any server. Your data is stored only in your browser, on your device.

Data you enter

The products you add (name, selling price, unit cost and units sold) are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there when you come back. This data stays on your device and is not shared with other devices or browsers.

Calculated values such as revenue, margins and menu engineering classifications are not stored; they are recalculated each time the page loads.

CSV files

When you import a CSV file, it is read and validated inside your browser and is not uploaded. Files you export or download are created in your browser and saved directly to your device.

Accounts

The current version has no accounts, sign-up or login.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current version does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it does not load fonts or scripts from third-party services.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser requests the pages, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure. This is handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement.

How to delete your data

You can delete products one by one in the tool, or remove all stored data by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Clearing your browser data, or using a private window, also removes it. Use Export CSV if you want to keep a copy.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled, such as analytics, advertising (for example Google AdSense), accounts or cloud storage, we will update this policy before those features are enabled and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, see the Contact page.
