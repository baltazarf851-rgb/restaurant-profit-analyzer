About Restaurant Profit Analyzer

Restaurant Profit Analyzer is a free, browser-based tool that helps you understand how much each item on your menu earns. You enter a few numbers for each product and the tool calculates the rest.

Who it is for

It is designed for owners, managers and anyone who manages the menu of a small restaurant, café, food truck or similar business, and who wants a quick view of menu profitability without setting up a spreadsheet.

What it does

  • Calculates revenue, product cost, gross profit and gross margin for each item and for the whole menu.
  • Calculates the contribution margin per unit and the popularity of each item.
  • Classifies items with menu engineering as Star, Plowhorse, Puzzle or Dog, and shows them on a matrix.
  • Imports and exports products as CSV files.

What it does not do

The calculations use only the selling price, unit cost and units sold that you enter. They do not include labor, rent, taxes or other operating expenses, so gross profit is not the same as the net profit of your business. The results are estimates to support your own analysis, not financial or accounting advice.

How your data is handled

The current version runs entirely in your browser and does not require an account. Your products are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Cost

All current features are free. An optional Pro version may be offered in the future; nothing about it has been decided yet.
