          How calculations work

          Profitability

          Revenue
          Selling Price × Units Sold
          Product Cost
          Unit Cost × Units Sold
          Gross Profit
          Revenue − Product Cost
          Gross Margin
          Gross Profit ÷ Revenue × 100

          Overall Gross Margin uses the totals of all products. Unit Cost is the estimated direct cost of one item, so these figures do not include labor, rent, taxes or other operating expenses.

          Menu Engineering

          Contribution Margin per Unit
          Selling Price − Unit Cost
          Average Contribution Margin
          Sum of contribution margins ÷ Number of products
          Popularity
          Product Units Sold ÷ Total Units Sold × 100
          Expected Popularity
          100% ÷ Number of products
          Popularity Threshold
          Expected Popularity × 70%

          An item has high contribution when its contribution margin is at or above the average, and high popularity when its popularity is at or above the threshold. Items are classified once at least one product has units sold.
